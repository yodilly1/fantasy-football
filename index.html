Keeper control room

All player values are auction dollars. League payments remain in NIS.

2-year max
  • Keeper windowA player may be kept for two additional seasons after the current team’s acquisition season. On the third possible keeper season, the player returns to the auction pool.
  • Trade resetA traded player receives a new keeper clock with the acquiring team. The system preserves the old team’s history but evaluates eligibility from the trade reset.
  • Current rule engineBase cost = last auction cost + $5. RB/WR floors and the ADP/median exception are applied after the base calculation.
  • What needs a voteAny change to the keeper tax, floors, number of keepers, or two-year maximum is versioned and tied to an effective season.
